# Limitations

- This submission does not show that inequality has been reduced. No community trial, adoption count, or testimonial was collected.
- The public deployment still fails session creation because it does not have a database. The PostgreSQL and private-storage code is in this working copy and has not been run against a real Supabase project. Local development still uses SQLite and `data/uploads`.
- Demo mode does not look at images or audio. The discriminating check used in the tests came from the rule fallback. It is a reasoned separation of the readings people typed, not a measured information gain and not a linguistic analysis.
- A session-verified concept means the session's own evidence rules were met. It does not mean the meaning is linguistically established.
- Two people agreeing, or a facilitator approving a check, does not verify a meaning.
- Independent drawings are stored as the mark coordinates in the response, not as a second image file. Photos and drawings for the discovery record still use the existing upload path.
- The same stimulus cannot be counted twice toward session verification. The selector does not search the whole space of possible checks, and it does not claim to.
- Community facilitator and participant are labels on the existing two seats. They are not an authority over the other person's language.
- The app asks for a display name and a session name. It is not a place for identity documents, medical details, or other sensitive personal information.
- SHORE-1001 is an illustrative sample with eight concepts. Its counts must not be presented as field observations.
