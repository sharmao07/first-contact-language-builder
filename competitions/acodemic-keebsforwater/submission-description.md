# First-Contact Language Builder

First-Contact Language Builder is a communication discovery system for two people who do not share a language. One person shows a place, an object, a gesture, or a preference. The other person answers from their own screen before either of them reads a suggested meaning. The session can then try a follow-up check that separates those readings.

The project already existed before this hackathon. The public repository was uploaded on 21 November 2026, and Speed Insights was added on 22 November 2026. The community-access work in this folder was made on 24 November 2026 and is still local. It has not been pushed or deployed.

## What a session does

- Community access, language research, or general discovery can be chosen when a session is opened. Community access is the guided path.
- The two roles are community facilitator and participant. Those names describe who guides the steps. Neither person decides what the other person's expression means.
- A situation such as asking for an everyday item moves through show, respond, compare, name what is unclear, run a check, and record the outcome.
- Responses stay private until both people lock them.
- A suggested check explains what is still missing and which readings each outcome would support or weaken. A person has to approve it. Approval does not mark a meaning as session-verified.
- Concept cards can be arranged on a shared board. The order is stored as a hypothesis, not as a grammar or a translation.
- A session record counts tasks, locked responses, checks, disagreements, and session-verified concepts from that session's own rows.

## What it does not do

It does not translate an unknown language, provide emergency or professional interpretation, or measure a reduction in inequality. Agreement between two people is a session record, not proof of a word.

Primary SDG: 10, Reduced Inequalities. Target 10.2, social, economic, and political inclusion. The relevant barrier is ordinary community interaction when people do not share a language.
